## 1. The game in one line

**Mixoloco is a cocktail merge puzzle: drop ingredients onto a bar table, merge matching ones into bigger and better ingredients, and mix each bar's cocktail menu before the table overflows.**

Short pitch (for store, press, ad intros):

> Drop, merge and mix your way across five dreamy bars, from a beach shack to a jungle hideaway. Merge cherries into vodka, vodka into mint, and keep going until you build each bar's grand finale. Mix the cocktails on the menu, shake the table when it gets crowded, and come back after dark for five night-time levels with new ingredients, new finales and new signature cocktails.

## 3. How it plays

1. **Drop.** The next ingredient waits at the near end of the table. Aim anywhere left to right and let go; it slides across the table to the far side.
2. **Merge.** Two identical ingredients that touch merge into the next, bigger ingredient in that bar's chain.
3. **Mix.** Each bar has a 7-cocktail menu. Make a cocktail's ingredients on the table to serve it; pick which one to chase from the Cocktail Menu.
4. **Finale.** Each level's chain ends in a grand final item (a jug, a champagne tower, a tequila barrel…).
5. **Level up.** Cocktail Mode on: complete all 8 tabs — every cocktail on the menu, plus the final item. Cocktail Mode off: merge two final items. On Level 10, merging two final items wins the game.
6. **Difficulty** ramps evenly across the 10 levels (each fills the table about 4% faster than the one before).
7. **Don't overflow.** If the pile reaches the line at the near end of the table, the game ends. You get a couple of extra throws to clear it, and a one-time continue by watching an ad.

**Power-ups** (bought with in-game credits):

| Power-up | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Cherry Bomb (renamed per bar, e.g. Sugar Bomb, Currant Bomb) | 50 | Clears every one of the bar's smallest ingredient |
| Shake | 100 | Shakes the table to jostle items into new merges |
| Mega-Shake | 400 | A bigger shake: items shrink and fly around; small ones scatter, big ones settle |
| Slam Mini-Shots | 300 | Clears every shot glass on the table (vodka, rum, gin, guaro…) |

**Other features**
- **Weekly league:** a ranked weekly leaderboard; your best score each week counts.
- **My Best:** your top scores, with the level and best evolution reached.
- **Guide:** every ingredient, cocktail (with real recipe steps) and song, unlocked as you play.
- **Music:** each bar has its own soundtrack. Day levels play 3 tracks; the night level unlocks the 4th.
- **Google sign-in:** backs up progress across devices.

## 5. Positioning

- **Category:** casual drop-and-merge puzzle, in the Suika / Watermelon Game family, with a cocktail-bar theme and goals (cocktail menus) layered on top.
- **What makes it different:** real cocktails with real recipes; five themed bars plus night versions; a soundtrack per bar; power-ups that change the table (shakes, bombs, clearing shots).
- **Tone:** bright, warm, playful, relaxing. Holiday-bar fantasy, not nightlife or partying.
- **Audience:** adults who play casual puzzle games (merge, match, word games) — broad age and gender mix; light, short sessions.
- **Comparable games** (for audience targeting): Suika Game / Watermelon Game, Merge Mansion, Royal Match, Two Dots, Wordscapes.

## 8. Rules: alcohol theme and age

- **Ad platforms restrict alcohol-related ads.** Keep every ad about the *game*: mixing, merging, puzzles. Use "mix", "merge", "serve", "make"; avoid "drink", "get drunk", "booze", "party" and anything that encourages drinking.
- **Age targeting:** 18+ everywhere, 21+ in the US. Some countries (notably India) limit alcohol-themed ads; expect lower delivery there.
- **Ads must be honest:** show real gameplay; don't promise features the game doesn't have.
- **South Korea:** the IARC rating came out as 19+, which Korea's rating board (GRAC) only grants directly. The game is not offered in Korea unless a GRAC rating is obtained later.
- **Store and YouTube:** mark videos "not made for kids". State "18+. No real alcohol" where it fits.

## 10. Campaign setup (Google Ads app campaign)

- **One ad group per language**, with the language setting matching its countries.
- **Audience signal:** custom segment of search terms (suika game, watermelon game, merge puzzle game, drop and merge game, fruit merge game, cocktail game, bartender game, relaxing puzzle game) and similar apps (section 5), plus the affinity "Casual & Puzzle Gamers". Add YouTube viewers and Firebase audiences once there's enough data.
- **Budget:** about 10× the target cost per install per day, so Google can learn. Leave a new campaign alone for 1–2 weeks (roughly 30–50 installs) before judging.
- **Creative:** text alone underperforms; always include the gameplay video (section 11).
